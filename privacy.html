Privacy Policy for Affan Ludo & Carrom

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Affan Ludo & Carrom ("the app", "we") is a free game with Ludo and Carrom played against computer opponents. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account, phone number, email address, or any login.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, such as your virtual coin balance, on your own device using local storage. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Virtual coins

Coins in the app are virtual items for entertainment only. They have no real-world value, cannot be bought, sold, or exchanged for money or prizes, and cannot be withdrawn.

Advertising and analytics

The app does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Online features

This version of the app does not send gameplay data over the internet. If online multiplayer is added in a future version, only the game moves needed to run a match will be exchanged, and this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
